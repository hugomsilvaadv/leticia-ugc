## Homepage architecture

### 1. Masthead

Optional descriptor: moda • beleza • lifestyle.

### 2. Lead editorial
One primary article with large photography.
Two to four secondary stories with varied editorial treatments.

### 3. Agora

- tendência
- semana de moda
- peça do momento
- lançamento
- styling idea

### 4. Moda

### 5. Beleza
Content types:
- maquiagem

### 6. Looks & Styling
Strong visual section based on Letícia's own photography.
Useful for both readers and prospective UGC clients.

### 7. Achados & Curadoria

Possible labels:
- Vale o clique

- Wishlist

### 8. Lifestyle
Travel, routines, places, personal style and selected motherhood content.

### 9. Most read / trending
Ranked list or visually compact module.
