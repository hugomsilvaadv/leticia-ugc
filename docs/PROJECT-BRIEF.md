# Project Brief — Letícia Leite

## Positioning
An authorial fashion and lifestyle publication with a creator behind it.

The site should not look like a traditional influencer media kit. The editorial experience is the primary product. The UGC business layer lives inside the same brand and benefits from the editorial authority created by the blog.

## Brand idea
Letícia's existing material repeatedly combines:
- fashion
- beauty
- lifestyle
- creativity
- product experience
- personal style
- communication
- femininity

Key existing phrases that may inspire future copy:
- "um conteúdo feito para conectar"
- "Moda, criatividade e comunicação para transformar produtos em experiências"

Do not hard-code a final slogan before brand copy is approved.

## Audience
Editorial audience:
- women interested in fashion, beauty and lifestyle
- digital consumers
- readers looking for looks, styling ideas, products, trends and practical inspiration

Commercial audience:
- authorial brands
- emerging brands
- beauty brands
- fashion brands
- e-commerce teams
- agencies and marketing teams looking for UGC

## Editorial model
Reference pattern: magazine-style homepage with clear editorial hierarchy, not a uniform card grid.

The publication should have:
- one dominant lead story
- secondary stories
- category-led content rails
- recent posts
- evergreen guides
- shopping/curation content
- beauty/review content
- visual lookbooks
- newsletter capture
- strong internal linking

## UGC integration
UGC should appear in four places:
1. top navigation as "UGC"
2. a restrained homepage block after editorial content
3. dedicated portfolio/services page
4. contextually inside relevant articles when useful

Avoid placing pricing on the homepage at launch.
